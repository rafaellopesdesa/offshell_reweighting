08 / what has been checked

Validate the likelihood before interpreting the curve.

01

Native likelihood

Extended RooFit likelihood, SR + 3 control regions, Gaussian constraints counted once. No SumW2 correction.

02

Independent evaluation

Explicit intensities, Poisson terms, and constraint penalties agree in ΔNLL to ≲2 × 10⁻¹⁰ in the completed construction and first-fit checks.

03

Physical profile points

Positivity is checked at the actual fitted points. A nuisance vector need not stay physical at every other μ.

04

Final acceptance

Full covariance, all 31 profile coordinates, overlap agreement, independent validation, and unchanged-input checks.

The figures show a likelihood scan, not confidence intervals. The publication uses Neyman confidence belts; q = 1 is not its 68% belt.
